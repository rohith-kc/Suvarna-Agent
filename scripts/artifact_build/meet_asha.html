Suvarna Gold Finance — a fictional demo lender

Meet Asha

Asha is a voice AI agent who calls customers who started a gold loan application and stopped partway through. She isn't selling — she's finding out why they stopped, and helping them finish if she can.

Built by Rohith KC for the Founding PM interview at Karta AI. Every lender fact, rate and figure here is a labelled placeholder — nothing is a real product.

Why "Suvarna"

Suvarna (सुवर्ण) is the Sanskrit word for gold — the same root Kannada, Hindi and most Indian languages still use for it today. A fictional lender, named plainly for what it lends against. Asha, the agent's name, means hope — short, easy to say on a call, and a reminder that her job is to get someone unstuck, not to sell them something.

Identity first

States who's calling and that she's an AI before any loan detail, every time.

No invented numbers

Every rupee amount comes from a calculator; she never does arithmetic herself.

Tested at scale

Checked against 25 scripted scenarios, including OTP traps and prompt injection.

Knows when to stop

Two tries on an objection, then a graceful exit — never a third push.

What this doesn't answer yet

  • Show-up rate. Whether a booked appraisal becomes a branch visit needs real lender data this demo doesn't have.
  • Consent and number series. India's telecom rules for which calls need which number series aren't confirmed — treated as promotional until counsel says otherwise.
  • Live integration. This runs against synthetic leads and a mock CRM, not a real loan origination system.
